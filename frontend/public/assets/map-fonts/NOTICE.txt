Glyph files (*.pbf) of the Offline basemap: Noto Sans Regular, Medium and Italic,
Latin ranges 0-255 and 256-511, as packaged by protomaps/basemaps-assets
(https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps-assets, fonts/, generated with
maplibre/font-maker). Noto Sans is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). Used by frontend/src/lib/offlineBasemap.ts.
